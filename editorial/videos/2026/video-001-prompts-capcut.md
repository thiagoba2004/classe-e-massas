# Prompts de geração — VÍDEO 001

**Ferramenta-base:** CapCut AI Video Generator  
**Uso:** gerar cada plano separadamente. Evitar pedir um filme inteiro em um único prompt.

## Prompt-base de continuidade

Use este núcleo em todas as cenas:

> Ultra-realistic cinematic social drama set inside a generic Brazilian bank branch, contemporary Brazil, no logos, no recognizable bank branding, no readable private data, natural human skin texture, realistic fluorescent and window lighting, restrained documentary cinematography, 24fps cinematic motion, subtle film grain, muted neutral palette, natural body language, no advertising look, no glossy corporate aesthetic, no text generated inside the image, consistent characters across shots.

### Personagens fixos
- bank worker: fictional Brazilian man, around 42, medium-brown skin, short dark hair, teal-blue button-down shirt without logo, dark trousers, generic unreadable ID badge;
- customer: fictional Brazilian man, around 55, gray polo shirt, ordinary appearance, impatient but not violent;
- branch manager: fictional Brazilian woman, around 45, beige blazer, calm and firm posture.

## Cena 01 — A espera

> Wide establishing shot inside a generic Brazilian bank branch late morning. Several customers wait seated, soft natural daylight mixed with modest fluorescent ceiling lights. The fictional 55-year-old customer in a gray polo checks his phone, looks toward the service desks, visibly impatient but controlled. Slow dolly-in, observational documentary camera, realistic Brazilian everyday environment, no bank logos, no readable signs, no text.

## Cena 02 — O trabalho invisível

> Medium close-up of the same fictional bank worker at his desk, carefully checking generic paper documents and switching between two computer screens. All screen content and documents must be intentionally blurred and unreadable. Hands type, compare fields, make a quiet internal phone call. Controlled slow camera movement, shallow depth of field, realistic office sound atmosphere, professional concentration, no brand logos.

## Cena 03 — Complexidade

> Cinematic inserts: worker verifies a generic ID card with all details blurred, looks at a secure screen with unreadable data, signs a generic internal checklist, pauses to think, then confirms something by phone. Natural facial micro-expressions, quiet tension, subtle handheld stability, 50mm lens feeling, realistic skin and paper texture, no readable personal information.

## Cena 04 — A fala pública

> Medium shot of the same customer standing from the waiting area and speaking louder toward the service zone, making a restrained open-hand gesture toward the worker. Other customers notice and turn their heads. The customer must not appear violent or threatening; the discomfort comes from public exposure. The worker remains seated in the background. Realistic social tension, natural reactions, no exaggerated acting, no text or logos.

## Cena 05 — O constrangimento

> Tight close-up of the same bank worker hearing the public complaint. He stays professional and silent, jaw slightly tense, eyes briefly shift toward the waiting area, then back to his desk. Background customers softly out of focus. Sound-world visually implied as if ambient noise lowers. Cinematic shallow depth of field, natural emotion, no melodrama.

## Cena 06 — A gestão intervém

> The same female branch manager in a beige blazer approaches the customer calmly, speaks quietly, gently indicates another service desk and redirects him there. She places herself between the public complaint and the original worker without physical confrontation. Respectful but firm body language. Smooth medium tracking shot, documentary realism, no logos, no generated subtitles.

## Cena 07 — Remanejamento e registro

> Cross-cut style shot: the customer is now calmly served by another generic employee at a different desk while the branch manager makes a short incident note on a computer with completely unreadable screen content. The original worker takes a controlled breath, organizes documents and resumes work. Natural office atmosphere, restrained emotional resolution, cinematic realism.

## Cena 08 — Normalidade com tensão residual

> Wide lateral shot of the bank floor returning to normal activity. Customers being served, workers focused, manager walking through the branch. The original worker visible in the mid-ground. Slow lateral camera move, realistic Brazilian bank environment, neutral natural light, subtle film grain, contemplative ending, no logo and no text. Leave clean negative space for editorial text added later in CapCut.

## Negative prompt / evitar

> distorted hands, extra fingers, duplicate people, deformed faces, plastic skin, cartoon look, luxury bank, futuristic bank, corporate commercial, exaggerated anger, physical violence, shouting face close-up, visible real bank logo, readable customer data, readable computer screens, generated captions, gibberish text, watermark-like fake logos, unrealistic camera movement.

## Montagem no CapCut

1. Gerar 2–3 variações por cena, se os créditos gratuitos permitirem.
2. Escolher primeiro a consistência dos personagens; só depois a beleza isolada do plano.
3. Usar cortes secos entre Cenas 01–05.
4. Usar transição muito discreta apenas entre Cenas 07–08.
5. Gravar a narração separadamente.
6. Inserir textos e cartelas somente no editor, nunca pedir ao gerador para desenhá-los.
7. Exportar em 1080p quando disponível; 4K somente se a mídia de origem e a conta/plataforma permitirem sem comprometer qualidade.
